Academic Projects

Swiss Films Festival

Creating a visual identity to promote the Swiss Films Festival 2024, and formulating a content strategy which glorifies the people working behind the scenes of film production to highlight the work that takes place beyond just what is shown on the big screens.

NPR Tiny Desk Concerts

Enhancing visitor experience to the site by implementing a filtering and searching system to streamline the discovery process of Tiny Desk Concerts from their expansive collection of live video performances.

HealthConnect

A health-tracking app for seniors which features a centralized system for medical information regarding individual patients, providing them with personalized health care recommendations and an assistive interface to reduce uncertainties regarding medical status and build confidence.

Food Bank on Wheels

A website redesign for Food Bank on Wheels to streamline the donor and client processes within the site. The newly implemented features allows clients to track their food hamper deliveries, while donors are able to access donation receipts for tax purposes and future reference.
